Commercializing a Dormant Strategic Asset

Portfolio Expansion Usage-Based Revenue Commercial Governance SMB Commercialization Regulated Market

Led end-to-end development of Ooredoo's first data monetization product from business case and regulatory clearance through API design, engineering delivery, and commercial launch, generating $100K in first-month revenue, exceeding forecast by 35%.

Context
Ooredoo is a fast-paced multi-national corporation offering telecom, cloud, and ICT services in 13 countries with 100M subscribers. Ooredoo Qatar is the head office operating in adherence with strict national consumer data privacy laws.
Problem
Ooredoo sits on a vast, untapped strategic asset in its consumer datasets. Meanwhile, SMBs were churning out of the Bulk SMS service due to a lack of customer data needed to run targeted campaigns — a missed revenue opportunity for Ooredoo on both sides.
Proposition
Unlock new revenue streams bridging both problems, by building a self-serve Marketing Campaigns as a Service platform allowing SMBs to launch demographic and location-based SMS and WhatsApp campaigns, powered by Ooredoo's anonymized customer data without compromising subscriber privacy.
Portfolio Expansion Strategy
Customer data was Ooredoo’s most underutilized strategic asset — a deep, anonymizable view of demographics, location, and behavior built up over years of subscriber relationships, sitting dormant inside the data warehouse with no commercial wrapper around it. The strategic intent was to expand the SMB portfolio beyond core connectivity and messaging into a new category — data-powered marketing services — and stand up a recurring, usage-based revenue stream that compounded with subscriber growth rather than competing against it. The objective was not a one-off product launch; it was opening a new portfolio pillar with a repeatable commercial playbook for future API and data offerings.
Target Segment & ICP Definition
The launch ICP was the active SMB Bulk SMS base — led by retail, hospitality, and healthcare verticals — customers already buying messaging from Ooredoo and visibly struggling to make it work. Their core friction was not channel cost: it was the inability to reach a relevant audience without first-party customer data they did not own. Enterprise customers were better resourced and had existing martech relationships; consumer was out of scope. SMBs were the right launch segment for three reasons — existing trust and billing relationship with Ooredoo, highest density of unmet demand for targeted reach, and a self-service buying behavior that matched the platform’s commercial model. Winning the SMB segment first would build the proof points needed to extend the offer upmarket and into adjacent data-monetization plays.

Business Case & Pricing Model: Developed a Business Case grounded in market analysis, opportunity cost, regulatory constraints, and MVP scope. Designed a pay-per-message pricing model with a premium for advanced targeting — aligning price directly to value delivered — and secured executive approval to fund the initiative.
Product Positioning: Reframed the offering from a commoditized "Bulk SMS" service to a value-added "Marketing Campaigns as a Service" — developing the pricing rationale, sales narrative, and training for Sales and Customer Support teams to position the product effectively at launch.
Regulatory and Privacy Clearance: This was the make-or-break risk for the product. Led the analysis with Legal and Regulatory teams, mapped compliance options with their trade-offs, and presented a recommended path to executives — securing formal approval that set a precedent for all future data monetization initiatives at Ooredoo.
Commercial Governance & Compliance: Built the commercial governance layer for the offer — consent capture across high-traffic touchpoints (service order forms, retail digital signing screens, base-wide consent SMS campaign) and new opt-out controls in the consumer mobile app — turning regulatory compliance into a defensible commercial moat and unlocking the legal right to monetize the underlying data asset at scale.
Offer Architecture & Commercial Guardrails: Designed the commercial offer around a parameterized audience configurator that gave SMBs self-serve access to targeting dimensions without exposing raw demographics — structured to add new audience attributes over time without re-engineering the buying flow or changing the price model. Built commercial guardrails — consent-flag filtering, frequency capping, and automatic audience redistribution — to protect subscriber experience, preserve brand trust, and safeguard the long-term viability of the revenue stream.
Solution Design: Designed a clean five-step self-service buying experience — select audience → write message → choose channel → pay → launch — deliberately shaped for commercial simplicity, low-touch adoption, and scalability across the SMB base. Underlying systems integration was abstracted away from the customer, keeping the offer consumable end-to-end without any sales-led handholding.

Spend Control Feature: Introduced a Campaign Spend Control feature letting SMBs dynamically resize their target audience to match their budget — directly addressing a key adoption barrier.

Launch Readiness & Channel Enablement: Drove cross-functional launch readiness ahead of commercial release — sales training and battlecards for SMB account teams, support readiness for tier-1/tier-2 inquiries, pricing readiness and quoting tools, escalation paths for billing and consent disputes, and operational governance across Product, Engineering, Legal, Marketing, and Commercial. Held formal cross-functional go/no-go reviews at each milestone to validate that every function was ready to commercialize the offer before flipping it live to the market.
Go-To-Market Launch Strategy: Partnered with Marketing to design and execute a multi-channel GTM launch — social media, email, SMS, and WhatsApp — with messages tailored to specific verticals (retail, hospitality, healthcare) and sequenced to land on the SMB base in the two weeks bracketing commercial availability.
Adoption & Activation Strategy: Designed the post-signup journey for fast adoption: a guided first-campaign onboarding flow that walked SMBs from signup to first campaign launched in under 15 minutes, a time-to-value playbook that surfaced audience-size and pricing trade-offs at the point of decision, and a repeat-usage strategy that prompted customers to relaunch around vertical-specific moments (retail promotions, hospitality seasonality, healthcare reminders). Customer Success owned a light-touch activation cadence for first-time users to remove friction and lock in second-campaign behavior, where retention economics compound.
Launch & Results: The first month delivered overwhelming validation. More than 50 SMB customers signed up within 30 days, 20% of whom launched campaigns more than once — generating $100K in revenue and exceeding the Business Case 30-day forecast by 35%.
Outcomes
  • Launched the first data monetization product at Ooredoo
  • Generated $100K in first-month revenue, 35% above forecast
  • Solved two commercial problems at once: SMB churn from Bulk SMS and the company’s long-stalled ambition to monetize dormant subscriber data
  • Established the regulatory, consent, and architectural precedent for every future data monetization initiative
  • Established the commercialization framework for future API and data monetization offerings — governance, consent management, pricing, and launch processes — reusable across the portfolio
  • Built a productized, scalable revenue engine from a previously dormant strategic asset
